Shovels Decisions are available to every account, in Shovels Online and through the API. This article covers plan access and coverage.

## Plan Access

| Plan | Decisions Access |
|------|------------------|
| **Free plan** | Included—search the last 12 months in Shovels Online, or query the decisions endpoints with your API key |
| **Paid plans** | Included at no additional charge, with full history in Shovels Online |

In [Shovels Online](https://app.shovels.ai), open the Decisions tab. See [How to search Decisions in Shovels Online](/docs/knowledge-base/shovels-online/searching-decisions).

If you have an API key, you can query the [Search Decisions](/docs/knowledge-base/api/decisions/searching-decisions) and [Get Decisions By ID](/docs/knowledge-base/api/decisions/decisions-by-id) endpoints today.

## Coverage

Decisions cover fewer jurisdictions than permits, and coverage continues to expand toward parity with permit data. Shovels collects new decisions daily and adds them to the dataset twice a month.

<Info>
Because coverage varies by jurisdiction, a geography with permit data may not yet have decision data. Check the [Coverage Dashboard](https://www.shovels.ai/coverage) for the latest.
</Info>

## Other Access Methods

Decisions are available in Shovels Online and through the Shovels API. For bulk or enterprise access, [contact sales](https://www.shovels.ai/contact).
