**The Shovels Free plan includes full permit, contractor, and decision search, all filters, and 500 credits per month.** No credit card required. Credits cover CSV downloads and API calls, and paid plans add full history and larger credit allowances.

<Info>
**One credit is one record.** Every plan is metered the same way—each record returned counts as one credit, on Free and paid plans alike.
</Info>

## Features Available on the Free Plan

### Permit, Contractor & Decision Search

Users can access **permit**, **contractor**, and **decision** search from the tabs above the results. See [How to search Decisions in Shovels Online](/docs/knowledge-base/shovels-online/searching-decisions) for the Decisions tab.

### Data Availability

- **Shovels Online**: The Free plan includes access to the last 12 months of permit, contractor, and decision data. Paid plans unlock all historical data (on average, 25+ years).
- **API**: The Free plan includes access to the full historical dataset—no date restrictions.

### Results You Can Browse

A search always reports how many records match, but the Free plan lets you page through the first **10** of them. Paid plans raise that to 100. Narrow your filters to bring a large result set into view, or export the results to CSV to work with more of them. See [how Shovels Online works](/docs/knowledge-base/shovels-online/how-it-works) for details.

### Filters and Sorting

Users can utilize various filters to refine their searches. Filters are available for categories such as:
- Financial
- Building
- Permit
- Contractor

You can export search results to CSV on the Free plan and sort them in Excel or Google Sheets. Each exported record spends one of your 500 monthly credits.

### Data Refresh Rate

Shovels data is refreshed twice a month. See [data refresh frequency](/docs/knowledge-base/data/quality/refresh-frequency) for details.

## Getting Started

1. [Create a free account](https://app.shovels.ai/login?mode=register) to get started
2. Use the search functionality to explore permits and contractors
3. Apply filters to narrow down your results
4. Explore contractor profiles and permit details

<Info>
The Free plan is a great way to evaluate Shovels' data quality and coverage before committing to a paid plan.
</Info>
