Shovels Decisions are categorized by the type of municipal action they represent. Understanding these categories helps you filter for the decisions most relevant to your use case.

Every decision has one category and may have a subcategory that narrows it down. The subcategories listed below are the ones Shovels defines for each category.

## Zoning Categories

### Spot Rezoning

A zoning change applied to a specific property or small group of parcels. Spot rezonings are typically initiated by property owners or developers seeking to change what can be built on their land.

**Subcategories include:** Mixed-Use Rezoning, Residential Rezoning, Special Use Permit

**Examples:**
- Changing a single-family residential lot to allow multifamily development
- Converting a commercial parcel to mixed-use
- Approving a special use permit for a specific activity at a known location

**Why it matters:** Spot rezonings often signal imminent development activity on that specific property.

### Area Rezoning

A zoning change applied to a larger geographic area, often initiated by the municipality as part of a comprehensive planning effort.

**Subcategories include:** Mixed-Use Rezoning, Commercial Rezoning, Industrial Rezoning, Retail Rezoning, Downtown Rezoning, Planned Unit Development (PUD)

**Examples:**
- Rezoning an entire corridor for transit-oriented development
- Upzoning a neighborhood to allow higher density
- Creating a new overlay district for a downtown area

**Why it matters:** Area rezonings indicate broader market shifts and can affect property values across many parcels.

### Zoning Code Modification

Changes to the zoning regulations themselves, rather than the zoning designation of specific properties. These modify what's allowed within existing zones.

**Subcategories include:** Limitations, Process Updates, Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs), Definitions Update, Short-term Rental, Extended Stay

**Examples:**
- Adjusting height limits in commercial zones
- Adding accessory dwelling units (ADUs) as a permitted use
- Changing the rules for short-term rentals citywide

**Why it matters:** Code modifications can unlock development potential across all properties in affected zones without individual rezonings.

## Land and Development Categories

### Annexation

A city or town extending its boundary to take in land that was previously outside it, such as unincorporated county land.

**Why it matters:** Annexed land often comes under new zoning and gains access to city services, which can make it buildable.

### Final Plat

Approval of a plat that divides land into lots, often the last planning step before a subdivision is built.

**Subcategories include:** Subdivision, Platting, Development Approval

**Why it matters:** A final plat approval is one of the clearest signals that new homes or lots are coming to a specific site.

### Project Amendment

A change to a development project that was already approved, such as a revised development plan.

**Subcategories include:** Development Plan Amendment

**Why it matters:** Amendments show that a known project is still moving, and often change its size, use, or timeline.

### Land Use Planning

Long-range planning decisions, including general plan amendments and the siting of schools and parks.

**Subcategories include:** General Plan Amendment, Elementary School, Middle School, High School, Education Center, Parks & Recreation

**Why it matters:** Plan amendments set the direction for future rezonings, and new schools and parks often draw residential development nearby.

## Public Investment Categories

### Affordable Housing

Decisions that create, fund, or require affordable housing.

**Subcategories include:** Affordable Housing Development, Inclusionary Zoning, Supportive Housing, Rent Assistance, Office-to-Affordable-Housing

**Why it matters:** These decisions often come with public funding attached, which moves housing projects forward.

### Infrastructure Development

Public infrastructure projects approved by the local government.

**Subcategories include:** Transportation, Water/Sewer, Water Management, Fire Department, Police Department, General Infrastructure

**Why it matters:** New roads, utilities, and public facilities open land for development and point to construction work for contractors in those trades.

### Economic Development Incentives

Grants, tax incentives, special districts, and business licenses the local government uses to encourage investment.

**Subcategories include:** Grants, Tax Increment Financing, Special Improvement Districts, Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ), Brownfield, Housing Program, Cannabis License, Liquor License

**Why it matters:** Incentives show where a city wants growth, and often attach to a specific project or business.

### City Properties

Transactions involving land or buildings the local government owns.

**Subcategories include:** Property Sale, Property Purchase, Property Lease, Property Transfer, Land Donation, Property Repurposing

**Why it matters:** City-owned sites that are sold, leased, or repurposed are often redeveloped soon after.

### Contract

Contracts the local government approves or amends, such as professional services agreements.

**Subcategories include:** Contract Amendment, Professional Services

**Why it matters:** Contracts identify the firms working for a city and the projects it is paying for.

## Filtering by Category

When searching for decisions, you can filter by category to focus on the types most relevant to your needs:

- **Development tracking:** Spot Rezoning, Final Plat, and Project Amendment
- **Market analysis:** Area Rezoning, Zoning Code Modification, Annexation, and Land Use Planning
- **Public investment:** Infrastructure Development, Affordable Housing, Economic Development Incentives, and City Properties
- **Comprehensive monitoring:** Include all categories

<Info>
Shovels Online shows categories as readable labels, such as Spot Rezoning. The API returns them as lowercase keys with underscores, such as `spot_rezoning`, and the API's category filter takes the same keys. The key for Project Amendment is plural: `project_amendments`.
</Info>
